About Us

Foreword

Makhanda Pulse is a Municple Sponserd Website Created to Aid the people of Makhanda in the Maintaince of the town by allowing for community members to report faults in the town's infrustructure.

Community members are also given notices to aid in the spread of information related to their wards and to help keep them safe from harm.

Purpose

This website serves as a way for the citzens to air issues to the municaplity, as a common problem has been getting incontact with people who can imform the community if there is progress on issues in Makhanda

In turn the municaplity now has a way to imform the community about dangers to be avioded and other important information that can effect the community.

Thanks

The end goal of this website is to improve Makhanda by allowing for open communication between the community and the municipal.

Thank you for learning about us and we hope that you become a registared member so that you can help restore our city.
