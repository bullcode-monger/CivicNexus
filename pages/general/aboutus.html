About Us

Makhanda Pulse is a Municple Sponserd Website Created to Aid the people of Makhanda in the Maintaince of the town by allowing for community members to report faults in the town's infrustructure.

Community members are also given notices to aid in the spread of information related to their wards and to help keep them safe from harm.

Thank you for learning about us and we hope that you become a registared member so that you can help restore our city.
